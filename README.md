Power House Gym & Fitness Center is located at Vardhmane House, Shahupuri, Kolhapur, Maharashtra, led by owner and head trainer Ameer Mullani (12+ years experience).

## 🚀 Quick Start (Instant Local Preview)

The built-in static HTTP server runs with zero external dependencies. Start it immediately with Node.js:

```bash
node server.js
```

Or using npm:

```bash
npm start
```

Then visit in your browser:
👉 **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)**

## ⚖️ Legal & Compliance Coverage (India DPDP Act 2023 & Liability Waiver)

1. **Mandatory Unbundled Consent Checkbox**:
   - Both the **Contact Consultation Form** (`/contact`) and the **Free Trial Booking Form** (`/visit`) require affirmative, user-checked confirmation:
     > *"I confirm that the details provided are accurate and consent to Power House Gym contacting me via WhatsApp, phone, or email. I have read and agree to the Terms of Service & Gym Rules and Privacy Policy."*
   - Forms cannot be submitted without actively accepting these terms.

2. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**:
   - Specified lawful purpose for processing personal data (trial scheduling and fitness consultations).
   - Data Fiduciary clearly identified: Ameer Mullani, Power House Gym, Shahupuri, Kolhapur.
   - User rights explicitly enumerated: Right to access, right to correction, right to erasure, and right to grievance redressal.
   - Strict zero-sale and zero-brokerage data policy.

3. **Fitness Health Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk (Indian Contract Act, 1872)**:
   - Medical clearance representation: Members certify they are in appropriate health for physical conditioning.
   - Voluntary assumption of risk for heavy resistance training and gym equipment.
   - Indemnification and release of liability for injuries or property loss.
   - Facility etiquette: Footwear requirements, towel hygiene, and mandatory re-racking of weights.
   - Clear refund terms (fees strictly non-refundable and non-transferable).

## 🎨 Interactive Features

1. **Brand Intro Video Overlay**: Plays `power-house-intro.mp4`/`.webm` on first visit with a `Skip intro` button and session storage tracking (`ph-intro`).
2. **Interactive Goal Selector**: Lets visitors click goals (*Weight Loss*, *Muscle Gain*, *Strength*, *Stamina*, *General Fitness*, *Active Lifestyle*) to see the corresponding fitness need, recommended training type, suitable plan, and exact pricing.
3. **Contact & Consultation Form**: Validates legal consent, collects client preferences, and automatically opens pre-filled WhatsApp chat with Ameer Mullani.
4. **Photo Lightbox**: Filterable spatial gallery with modal dialog preview for high-resolution images.
5. **Mobile Quick Action Bar**: Fixed bottom bar on mobile viewports for quick Call, WhatsApp, and Free Trial booking.
