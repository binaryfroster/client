DATA TRANSPARENCY & ETHICS

COOKIE & STORAGE POLICY

Honest disclosures on cookies, local storage, and privacy preservation at Power House Gym Kolhapur.

Core Commitment: Power House Gym & Fitness Center values your digital rights and user experience. We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site trackers or sell user behavior data. This policy explains what browser storage technologies we utilize and how you can manage them.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are minute alphanumeric data files placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies include HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage, which allow websites to preserve your preferences locally in your browser without communicating unnecessary identifiers across the network.

2. Our Approach to Cookies

Unlike ad-supported websites, Power House Gym is a fitness facility located in Shahupuri, Kolhapur. Our website exists strictly to showcase our gym equipment, training programs, coach credentials, and trial booking options. Consequently:

  • We do not use invasive third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels (such as Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel).
  • We only employ storage essential for smooth interface operation, session integrity, and user preference persistence.

3. Categories of Storage We Use

A. Strictly Necessary & Functional Storage

These elements are required for core website features to operate correctly. They cannot be deactivated without impairing the performance of the website.

  • phg_cookie_consent_v1: Stores your chosen cookie consent preferences (Essential Only or Accept All) so you are not prompted repeatedly on subsequent visits. Retained locally for up to 12 months.
  • ph-intro: Remembers whether you have already seen or dismissed the initial brand introduction video, preventing repetitive playback.
  • Form Session Caching: Temporarily retains selected training programs or consultation time slots as you navigate between pages.

B. Third-Party Embedded Media & Maps

To help you locate our facility in Shahupuri, we integrate a secured Google Maps embed. Google may set technical cookies to remember map zoom preferences and bandwidth conditions. These embeds are hardened with modern sandbox and no-referrer attributes.

C. Performance & Analytics (Non-Intrusive)

If enabled, aggregated, anonymized visit telemetry measures which pages (such as Membership Programs or Personal Training) are most visited to help us optimize server performance and mobile responsiveness. No personal identifiers are recorded.

5. How to Manage Your Preferences

You maintain complete control over how cookies and storage are handled on your device:

Additionally, all major web browsers provide built-in controls to block or purge cookies at any time:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.
