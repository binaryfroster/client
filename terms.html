4. Facility Code of Conduct & Gym Etiquette

To preserve a focused, safe, and hygienic training environment for all members, the following rules are strictly enforced:

  • Footwear Mandatory: Clean indoor athletic training shoes are mandatory on the training floor at all times. Outdoor sandals, slippers, or bare feet are strictly prohibited for safety and hygiene.
  • Re-Racking Weights: Every member must return all dumbbells, barbells, weight plates, and attachments to their designated racks immediately after finishing a set. Leaving weights on bars or floor is strictly prohibited.
  • Hygiene & Towels: Members must carry a personal workout towel and wipe down machine seats and benches after use.
  • Equipment Safety: Dropping dumbbells or loaded barbells from waist/shoulder height onto unpadded floor sections is prohibited. If you need a spotter, request assistance from Ameer or gym staff.
  • Respect & Decorum: Mutual respect between members and trainers is fundamental. Zero tolerance for abusive language, intimidation, harassment, or commercial solicitation.

5. Membership, Payments & Refund Policy

  • Advance Payment: All membership dues (Regular Membership, Personal Training, Diet Add-on) must be paid in full prior to facility access.
  • Strictly Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable: Once activated, memberships are strictly non-refundable and cannot be transferred to any third party under any circumstances.
  • Personal Training Sessions: PT sessions are tailored one-on-one appointments with Ameer Mullani. In the event of a scheduled cancellation, a minimum of 24 hours advance notice is required to reschedule, failing which the session will be forfeited.
