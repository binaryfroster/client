The Difference Between Simply Sweating and Truly Progressing

Most gym members abandon their fitness journey within 90 days. It is rarely due to a lack of willpower—it is usually because they are left guessing on the gym floor, struggling with poor posture, repeating empty sweat sessions, or fighting nagging joint pain from lifting with incorrect mechanics.

At Power House Gym, Personal Training is not a luxury sales package; it is an active coaching partnership. Your trainer stands right next to you for every single set. We adjust your foot stance by an inch, correct your elbow flared angle on a bench press, control your tempo, and calibrate your weights so you break through plateaus safely and methodically.

100% Active Guidance Direct hands-on posture, tempo, and rep monitoring on every single set.
Zero Wasted Effort No guesswork, random machine surfing, or risking preventable injuries.
